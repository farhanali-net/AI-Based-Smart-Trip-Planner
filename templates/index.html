About The Project

AI-Based Smart Trip Planner is a University Project that helps tourists discover the most suitable travel destinations in Northern Pakistan. Instead of manually searching for destinations, users simply provide their travel preferences and the AI recommends the best location along with a complete day-wise itinerary.
